ReferMe Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("ReferMe," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the ReferMe mobile app for iPhone, iPad, and Android and related websites (the "Service"). ReferMe is a referral app: consumers recommend friends to vendors and salespeople and can earn rewards when a referral becomes a lead or a sale. We do not provide, schedule, or book the vendors' products or services.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password in plain text), and account type (consumer or vendor).
  • Profile information: profile photo, bio, your list of "Preferred vendors," friends/connections, and posts you publish.
  • Vendor business information: business name, industry, service area, contact details, reward offers, and, for company accounts, team/salesperson seats.
  • Referral information about other people: when you refer someone, you give us their name, phone number and/or email, and notes about what they are looking for. Only submit someone's details if they have agreed to be contacted by the vendor.
  • Messages and support: in-app messages with vendors or users, dispute details, and support tickets you send us.
  • Photos: images you choose to upload (for example a profile photo). We only access photos you select.

Information created as you use the Service

  • Referral and reward activity: referrals made and received, their status (lead, closed sale), reward points, reward obligations, and gift cards issued.
  • Wallet and transaction records: wallet balances, holds, top-ups, and reward payments recorded in the app (we do not store full card or bank numbers).
  • Account health and standing: for vendors, an account health score, payment standing, and badges (such as "Trusted" or "New vendor") calculated from payment history.
  • Invite tracking: your personal invite code/link and which accounts joined through it, so we can credit referrals.
  • Device information: push-notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service) and basic technical data our hosting providers log (such as IP address and timestamps) for security and operation.
  • Subscription status: which plan you are on and its renewal status.

We do not use third-party advertising, ad tracking, or cross-app tracking, and we do not collect your precise location or your phone's contacts list.

2. Payments

  • Vendor payouts and reward payments: processed by Stripe (Stripe Connect). Stripe collects identity and bank details needed for payouts and verification ("know your customer"). This information goes directly to Stripe under the Stripe Privacy Policy; we receive only limited information such as account status and transaction records.
  • Plan subscriptions: purchased through Apple In-App Purchase on iOS/iPadOS or Google Play Billing on Android. Apple or Google processes your payment; we receive confirmation of your purchase and subscription status, not your card details.
  • Gift card rewards: delivered through [GIFT CARD PROVIDER], which receives the recipient's name and email to deliver the card.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account and provide the Service.
  • Send referrals to the vendor you chose, track their status, and calculate and pay rewards.
  • Maintain vendor accountability: reward payment reminders, account health scores, badges, and restrictions for unpaid rewards.
  • Send transactional emails and push notifications (for example, new leads, reward updates, payment reminders).
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse (for example, vendors cannot earn rewards on their own business), and comply with law, including tax reporting where required.
  • Understand and improve the Service using aggregated usage data.

4. How information is shared

  • With vendors: when you make a referral, the vendor receives the referred person's contact details and your name so they can follow up and reward you.
  • With other users: your public profile, Preferred vendors, and posts are visible to other users.
  • With company owners: on company (Elite) accounts, the owner can see their salespeople's seat, standing, and card-charge information.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase / Google Cloud (hosting, database, authentication, file storage, cloud functions, push notifications); Stripe (payments and payouts); Apple and Google (subscriptions and notifications); [EMAIL PROVIDER: Resend or SendGrid] (email delivery); [GIFT CARD PROVIDER] (gift cards). They may use the data only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the Service.
  • Business transfers: in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Retention

We keep account information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within [30] days, except records we must keep for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute purposes (such as reward payment and transaction records), which we keep only as long as required. Referral contact details are kept while the referral is active and deleted or de-identified afterwards per our retention schedule.

6. Your choices and account deletion

  • You can view and edit your profile in the app.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Menu → Settings → Delete account) or by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL].
  • You can turn off push notifications in your device settings. Transactional emails needed to operate your account cannot be turned off while the account is active.
  • If someone referred you and you don't want to be contacted, email us and we will remove your details.

ReferMe is not directed to anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If we learn that someone under 16 has provided us information, we will delete it and close the account. If you believe this has happened, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].
